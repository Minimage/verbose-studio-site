How to print it
  1. Print double-sided (on both sides of the paper).
  2. Choose flip on short edge. Some printers call it short-edge binding. With long edge, every back side comes out upside down.
  3. Use Letter paper and print at actual size (100%).
  4. Keep the sheets in the order they come out, fold the stack in half and staple along the fold.
