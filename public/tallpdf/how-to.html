How to split a long, tall PDF into regular pages

Five quick steps. It all happens in your browser, so your file never leaves your device.

Hover a picture to watch the stepTap a picture to watch the step
  1. Step 1

    Open your PDF

    Drop the tall PDF onto the page, or press Open a PDF and pick it.

  2. Step 2

    Pick a page size

    Choose Letter, A4 or your own size. Leave Cuts on Automatic and the tool finds the gaps for you.

  3. Step 3

    Move a cut if you want

    Drag any dashed line to a better spot. Its label says clean when it sits in a gap.

  4. Step 4

    Leave a section out

    Put a cut above and below anything you don't want, then untick its box. It is skipped in the PDF.

  5. Step 5

    Download your PDF

    You get a normal multi-page PDF. Print it or share it like any other.
