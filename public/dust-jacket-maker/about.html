About Dust Jacket Maker

A small free tool for one common job.

Why it exists

A dust jacket is simple: one long strip of paper with five panels. Getting the sizes right is the fiddly part, and ready-made templates only come in a few book sizes. This tool works the panels out from your own measurements and gives you a PDF at the real size, with the marks a print shop or a home printer needs.

What it does

It lays out the back flap, back cover, spine, front cover and front flap from your measurements, adds a wrap allowance and optional bleed, places your images on the panels, and draws trim and fold marks outside the artwork. It makes one full-size page, or tiles the same jacket over Letter or A4 sheets with an overlap and matching marks.

What it does not do

  • It does not design the cover for you. Bring your own images, or use flat colours and spine text.
  • It does not put text on the back cover or the flaps. Add blurbs to the images in your design app first.
  • It does not do CMYK or spot colours. The PDF is in ordinary screen colours (RGB), which home printers and most print shops handle fine.
  • Spine text uses one plain bold font. Text in other alphabets is drawn as a picture, so it still prints.

Who makes it

One person makes and looks after this site. It is a small project, kept simple on purpose.

Your files

Your images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. The privacy page explains what the site does and does not store.
